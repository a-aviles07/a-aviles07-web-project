Top Marvel Movies

Marvel movies began in the early 2000s but really took off in 2008 when the first Iron Man movie was released. After this, it went on to become one of the most popular movie franchises in the world.

The Marvel Cinematic Universe has about 37 theatrical films and over 30 television series that includes specials and shorts!

Marvel movies are some of the most popular films in the world within the Superhero category. Here are the top 5 Marvel movies, based on opinion, today.

Top 5 MCU Movies

  1. Iron Man
  2. Captain America: Civil War
  3. Avengers
  4. Spiderman: No Way Home
  5. Thunderbolts
